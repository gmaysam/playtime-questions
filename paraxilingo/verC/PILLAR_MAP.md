## Scoring

- Skill score = right-first-try rate over its last 30 answers, smoothed ((right+1)/(tries+2)), level weights easy 0.85 / normal 1 / hard 1.2.
- Exploring < 6 answers or 1 day · Practising 6+ answers on 2+ days · Showing progress score ≥ 70 with 10+ answers.
- Pillar score = √n-weighted mean of skills with enough data; needs 10+ answers, else “Not enough data”.
- Last-week / last-month radar outlines only when 3+ pillars had scores then.
- Answers recorded before Nov 10 come from the per-skill history (last 10 per skill), dated on the day that skill was last played.
