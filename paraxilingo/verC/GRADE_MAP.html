Paraxilingo Grade Map

Every question type in the app has a school grade, taken from Khan Academy Kids and PBS KIDS (preschool to grade 2) and Khan Academy (grades 3 to 6). A child sees a type from the grade it is taught until one or two grades later. The child's grade is their age minus 5, moved one grade down for 🐢 and one up for 🚀.
